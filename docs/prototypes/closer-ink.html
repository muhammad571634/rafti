Closer, the Ink way

The level-up after a chat, rebuilt from the canvas frame: black ground, serif words, one ring in her colour. No confetti or sunburst any more.

  1. The number rolls 4 → 5, the line under the title draws.
  2. The ring draws with a bright spark at its tip; her face fades in inside it.
  3. When the ring closes: one soft echo, the chime and one success tap on the phone, all at the same instant.
  4. Her new stage appears in her colour; on a story level the chapter name and «Read it» (back to the chat, where she tells it). Other levels: «Mia feels a little closer to you» and «Continue».
  5. Then the ring breathes slowly, like the ring on Today.

Reduced motion: everything simply fades in, the ring is shown full.
