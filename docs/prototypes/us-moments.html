Our moments: three ways

Today every row says only what kind of thing it was ("Exchanged secret notes"), so rows look like copies and there is nothing to remember. All three options fix the logic the same way; they differ only in how it looks.

  • Each moment is tied to its own note, date, call or photo: the row shows their words, the hearts or the length, and a tap opens exactly that one.
  • A plan shows on the day it happens (with its time), not on the day it was made; upcoming plans stay in the calendar above, so nothing is listed twice.
  • Big moments (you met, a new level, a chapter opened) stand out from everyday ones.
  • Four filters that fit the screen (All, Together, Words, Milestones) instead of seven that run off it.
