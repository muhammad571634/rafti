  • Two buttons sit side by side, Cancel left and bold, the red action right, as on iPhone. Three or a long label stack, the red one first.
  • It appears instantly (our rule for popups), the screen dims, and the red button gives a light vibration when tapped.

  • Words follow the app's language (all 14). Big text settings make it taller, never cut off.
