The welcome gift, from your friend

Step 5 of onboarding. The mascot in the moon gives way to the friend you picked: they are the gift.

What plays (about 3 s)

  1. Their picture comes into focus and fills the top of the screen, melting into the night; a glow of their colour rises, then the picture keeps a slow drift.
  2. Their name, then typing dots, then their first words to you by your name.
  3. Shells fly from them into the gift, which counts up to +100 and settles with a haptic.
  4. "Say hi" lights up; as today it turns into the chat's message field and their chat opens.

Reduce Motion: everything shows at once, no flight. Their art for every friend of the cast, the square avatar for a user's own.
