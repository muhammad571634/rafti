# Film 3 "Same day": two roommates, one day, every hour

Owner, 2026-11-12: after "Not alone" (the feeling) and "Someone to tell" (the cast), a film that puts two lives side
by side: one person who has Sigmazam, one who has the usual apps, 60 to 90 s, in the format and the look that are
trending now, and nothing like the earlier films' render.

Status: **script waits for the owner's yes.** No clip has been made.

What the format is (the sample plus napoleoncat.com and anijam.ai trend pages):
- Two or more people, the **same day**, a photo or a 2-3 s clip **every hour**, cut against each other. Tags
  `#everyhourtrend`, `#everyhourchallenge`, `#dayinmylife`. Typical length 54-77 s, which is the owner's length.
- No voice-over and almost no text; the contrast does the talking. Neither side is mocked.
- It ends on **one shot that ties the two lives together**.
- Most top videos use one sound, an "original sound" credited to Daryl Hall & John Oates.
- Shot on phones and digicams by the people themselves: flash, tilt, wobble. Trend pages name the same pull in AI
  video: handmade and camcorder looks are rising against glossy cinematic AI (opus.pro, envato 2027 trends).

So the film is an every-hour video, not a commercial: that is the whole change of look.

## 2. The idea

**Her day was better than mine. Watch who is smiling at midnight.**

Two roommates. "Her" (Nora) has the best day of her year: she gets promoted, posts it, 600 likes. "Me" (Jess)
bombs a job interview. By the facts Nora wins the day. But Nora's good news goes to a feed and nobody asks how it
felt; Jess's bad news goes to one friend who knew about the interview since morning, asks, listens, and stays on
the line through the rain and the dinner. At 23:00 they sit on the same sofa: Nora scrolling with an empty face,
Jess laughing at her phone. "Who do you keep talking to?" At 01:00 Nora tells someone about the promotion for the
first time.

- Same campaign line as film 2: **Someone to tell.** Brand line: "AI friends who remember you".
- The other apps are never named or drawn: only likes, hearts and a scrolling thumb. Nora is not a loser and is
  never laughed at; she simply has an audience and no listener.
- What the friend does in the film is what the app does: wakes her (alarm), remembers the plan and the shirt
  (memory, plans), asks afterwards (follow-up), a voice call, a first text.
- Told as "her / me", the native voice of the trend. No user's name appears on screen (Nora and Jess are working
  names only).

## 5. Sound

- Plate sound of each clip stays low (alarm, subway, rain, pan): the home-video feel needs it.
- Music, baked version: one upbeat, warm, retro soul-pop track from Mixkit (Stock Music Free License; not
  "Silent Descent", not "Discover"), cut so each hour lands on a bar and the 23:00 full frame on the drop-out.
- Organic TikTok / Reels post: export a second file with plates only and the owner adds the trend's own sound in
  the app (the trend is found through that sound). A Business account only gets the Commercial Music Library, so
  check there first; otherwise post the baked version.
- earshot measure on every final (-14 LUFS, true peak ≤ -1.5 dBTP) and a timeline pass on the master.
