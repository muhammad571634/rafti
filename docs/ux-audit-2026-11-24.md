# Whole-app UX audit (2026-11-24, Claude 1)

Owner's ask: "find every error and every bad place on every screen; where and how we make it better, and
how to do it easily". Sources: 40 screenshots on the A51 today (Today, Chats, a chat with a reply, Us, Find
scrolled to the end, Stories, Gifts, Store, History, My plan, Profile, Edit profile, Notifications and their
settings, Personas, World books, Keeping in mind, Contacts, Calls, Alarms, Bedtime, Radio, Board, Photo
Booth, Diary, Dates, Couple quiz, New group, Create, Language, a friend's profile, settings, memories, chat
background), the colour audit (`docs/color-audit-2026-11-24.md`) and HIG. Colour items already fixed today
are not repeated.

Effort: **S** = under an hour, code only; **M** = a few hours; **L** = a day or art/owner input.
"Owner" = a visible design change that needs the owner's yes first (design freeze, approved screens).

## P0: hurts the first minutes or the product's first job

| # | Where | Problem | Better, and the easy way | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Chat (seen twice today) | **"Not Delivered"** after a user's line: the free Gemini key answers 503 "high demand"; the friend goes silent. The product's first job is a friend who answers. | The paid key (owner). Until then: retry once more after 8-10 s before "Not Delivered", and show "Milo is busy, I'll answer soon" instead of a red error on the first failure. | S (retry) / owner (key) |
| 2 | Find → Stories (8 cards on Find, 100 on See all) | **Empty 3:4 letter cards** ("L", "N", "A", "J"): half the Find page is big flat gradients with one letter, every one tagged "New". It reads unfinished. | Portraits (owner, Nano Banana, 12-16 first). Easy now: until a template has a picture, show a **compact text card** (small round letter avatar beside the name, hook and tags, no 3:4 block), so 4-6 stories fit where 2 fit now; hide "New" when every card would say it. | S (compact card), L (art) |
| 3 | Find (whole page) | **Too long and mixed**: hero carousel, Who do you miss, Stories with 10 chips, See all, From the community (one half-width card, empty right half), Relationships folders, Groups. Hard to scan; the tab bar covers the last row. | One idea per section: keep hero + Stories; move "Who do you miss?" into the + (Create) menu or under Stories; community as a horizontal row of full cards; folders last. | M, owner |

## What is good and should stay

Calm night ground, one peach for actions, real character art on Today / chat / profile, iOS-style lists
(Language, Settings, Notifications), the tab bar, typography roles (serif titles, system body), the chat
bubbles now (peach yours with dark text, quote readable at 7.5:1), Memories, Couple quiz, Radio, Photo Booth.

## Suggested order

1 (retry, S) → 2 (compact story card, S) → 6, 7, 11, 12, 16, 17 (S, no owner input) → owner picks for
3, 4, 5, 8, 15, 18 → portraits (owner) → 14.
