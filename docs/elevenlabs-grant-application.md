## Draft answers (English)
**Company:** [name], [country], solo founder, 1 employee. Website: [url].

**Product:** Sigmazam is a mobile app (iOS first, Android after) where adults 18+ chat, text, send voice messages and call AI characters: 55 original characters in 7 story worlds, with memory, a shared story and group chats, in 14 languages. All characters are labelled as AI. No real people, no copyrighted characters.

**Why voice:** a character that answers in a believable voice is the product's main retention and paid feature. We tested ElevenLabs against Fish Audio and Gemini TTS in a blind test in five languages; ElevenLabs sounded clearly more natural and clean, so we want to build on it.

**Plans (monetization):** Free (daily free shells, pre-recorded voice letters), Basic $9.99 a month (chat), Pro $29.99 a month (chat, 120 minutes of voice replies, 180 minutes of calls), Pro yearly $239.99. Extra minutes are sold for in-app shells. Daily minute caps protect margins. Modelled margin per Pro user at typical usage is positive; the grant covers the launch period.

**ElevenLabs use:** TTS (Flash for calls and live voice, v3 for voice messages), later Agents for live calls and Speech to Text for voice messages. Estimated 3 to 10 million characters a month in the first year.

**Safety:** 18+ only, age gate, AI disclosure, crisis-help card, reporting, no cloning of real people (own voice only, with explicit consent), content within ElevenLabs' use policy.

**Stage and traffic:** [pre-launch / test build on a real phone]; target 100 to 300 paying users in the first months.
