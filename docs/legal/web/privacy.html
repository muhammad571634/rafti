Who can use Sigmazam

Sigmazam is for adults, 18 and over. The app asks for your year of birth during onboarding on your device and does not let anyone younger in. If you believe someone under 18 is using Sigmazam, write to support@sigmazam.app and we will delete the account.

The short version

  • You do not need an email or phone number to start; your account is created anonymously on your device.
  • Your chats are processed by our AI service to write characters' replies. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party ad tracking.
  • You can delete your account and everything we keep for it inside the app at any time in Profile → Delete account.

What we collect

Account & preferences. An anonymous account ID, your language and time zone (so days, free shells, and greetings follow your clock), and the display name you choose for chats. Your birth year is checked on your phone and stays there.

Chats & memories. When Chat backup is enabled (turned on after your AI consent, and toggleable anytime in Profile → Chat backup), your one-to-one and group messages, voice-message transcripts (audio recordings stay on your device), short descriptions of photos you share (raw photos are not stored on our servers), and character memories are backed up so your conversations can be restored.

Shells & plans. Your shell balance, earned or spent shells, daily check-in state, and subscription plan.

Community & reports. Characters you choose to publish to the community catalogue, and any message reports (the reported message, reason, and up to 8 preceding lines for safety review).

Usage & app activity. Per-request model/voice counts and cost records (holding no message content) and aggregate in-app activity events (screens visited, call lengths, levels reached—never message text or names). Turn off app activity anytime in Profile → Share usage data.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, advertising ID, or browsing history.

Service providers

  • Google (Gemini API): writes character replies, transcribes voice messages, and understands photos you send (only after your in-app consent). On our paid API tier, Google does not use prompts to train or improve its products.
  • ElevenLabs: synthesizes live call voice lines when voice calls are enabled; only signed character lines are sent and no user chats are stored there.
  • Supabase & Fly.io: store our database and run our API server in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Apple & Google: deliver app updates, push notifications, and store subscriptions.

How long we keep data

  • Account, chats, memories, and shells: until you delete your account.
  • Safety reports: automatically purged after 12 months, or immediately on account deletion.
  • Usage records & app activity events: automatically purged after 13 months, or immediately on account deletion.
  • Daily anti-abuse rate counters: automatically purged after 7 days.

Your rights & choices

Delete your account anytime in Profile → Delete account. Toggle cloud backup in Profile → Chat backup and activity counts in Profile → Share usage data.

Depending on where you live (including the EU, UK, and California), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or object to processing. Write to support@sigmazam.app and we will respond within 30 days.

AI characters

Characters are AI, not real people. Do not rely on a character for medical, legal, financial, or crisis advice. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number immediately.
